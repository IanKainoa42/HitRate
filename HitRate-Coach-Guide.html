A step-by-step guide to logging practice, reading your dashboard, and unlocking Stunt Cards for your team.

Getting Started

Set Up Your Team in 3 Steps

When you first open HitRate, you'll walk through a quick setup to create your team's card deck. Nothing is pre-made — you build a roster that matches your program.

Name Your Deck

Type your team or program name — this becomes your folder. Tap "My team's deck" to use coach language (groups, program name on cards) or "Just mine" for athlete mode (skills, your own name). You can also enter your program name here.

Mint Your Cards

Pick a category — Stunts, Tumbling, Jumps, or Tosses — and tap the suggestion chips to add skills (Prep, Extension, Full Up, etc.), or type your own custom name. Each skill you add becomes a card in your deck.

Log Your First Rep

A quick practice preview lets you try logging a few reps before landing on your dashboard. One tap per rep — you'll be up and running in under a minute.

Tip

Already used HitRate before? Tap Sign in with Apple/Google on the first screen to restore your previous folders and data.

At Practice

Logging Reps

The Counter (Freeform Mode)

Tap the green "Start practice" pill on your Home screen. The counter opens full-screen with your skills as rows and outcomes as columns. Logging is one tap — tap the outcome cell for the skill you're watching, and the rep is saved instantly.

A 3.5-second undo toast appears after each tap in case you mis-log. Your recent reps show as chips at the bottom of the screen.

To create your first page: long-press (or context-menu) the practice pill → "Run or build a page." Once pages exist, the practice pill gives you a choice: Freeform Counter, Run a Page, or Build/Edit Pages.

When running a page, arm an outcome (Hit, Bobble, etc.) in the bar at the top, then tap any pocket to log that outcome for that skill.

The Pivot Toggle (Skill ↔ Athlete)

At the top of the counter there's a pin toggle. It changes what's pinned at the top vs. what varies row-by-row:

  • Pin a skill → rows become athletes (subjects). Log each person's attempt on the same skill.
  • Pin an athlete → rows become skills. Log one person's reps across multiple skills.

You can add athletes or skills on the fly during practice — name them now or later.

Tip

Have an Apple Watch? HitRate's watch companion acts as a remote clicker — scroll skills with the Digital Crown, tap to log. The watch wakes automatically when you start practice.

Your Dashboard

Reading the Numbers

After practice, the Home screen is your analytics hub. Everything updates live as reps are logged. Use the timeframe tabs at the top — Today, This Week, or All-Time — to change the window.

Summary

Your headline clean-hit rate with a distribution bar. The big number is hits ÷ total reps — a bobble is not a hit.

Trend

A line chart of your rate over time. The delta badge shows change vs. your baseline (yesterday, last week, or start of season).

Skills Leaderboard

All skills ranked by hit rate with a heatmap bar. Quickly see which skills are strong and which need work.

Skill Report

Your best, worst, cleanest, and most consistent skills ranked automatically (6+ reps minimum).

Session Tape

A visual barcode of your latest session — each bar is one rep, color-coded by outcome. A highlighted region marks your rough patch (worst stretch of 7 consecutive attempts). Great for spotting when energy dipped.

Competition

The Trophy Room

Tap the trophy icon on the Home header to open the competition hub. HitRate runs a weekly tournament across your skills — no setup needed, it's automatic.

Three Rotating Cups

Each week, one of three games is in play. The game rotates every Monday:

Longest landing streak

The Spirit

A synthetic pace-setter that competes every week at the average winning score of past weeks — your team's bar to beat. Shown as a dashed ghost entry on the leaderboard. If no one beats The Spirit, it takes the cup. Use it to motivate athletes to push past "good enough."

The Trophy Room also shows the Season League (placement points accumulate all season: 5 for first, 3 for second, 2 for third, 1 for qualifying) and a Cups Won grid of past weekly champions. Season resets each June.

Share & Celebrate

Stunt Cards

Every skill on your roster has a collectible card that evolves with work. Open the cards from the Home action row. Cards are designed for sharing on Instagram Stories and social media.

The Card Ladder

Cards level up based on total reps and achievements — never by rate alone:

MINTED 0 reps
INKED 1+ reps
PROVEN 50+ reps
DECORATED Badges earned
✨ FOIL Legendary badge

Badges appear as pips on the card — volume milestones (100 / 500 / 1000 reps), best hit-run streaks (10 / 25), mastery milestones, and weekly cup wins. A legendary badge upgrades the entire card to a holographic foil.

Milestone Cards

In addition to stat cards, the app unlocks milestone cards for achievements — volume records, long streaks, perfect sessions, skill mastery, and even "dubious honor" cards for fall streaks. These unlock automatically from your rep history — no setup needed. Earned milestones can also be saved as round cheer pucks (collectible badge images).

Sharing

From the card deck: share directly to Instagram/Messages, save to Photos, or copy image to paste into any app. Locked milestone cards (not yet earned) can't be shared — they show the progress needed to unlock.

Between Practices

Homework (Weekly Rep Targets)

Assign weekly rep targets to your athletes so work between practices is visible.

How It Works

  • From the Home screen, open the Homework card or the editor to create an assignment.
  • Pick a skill, set a rep target (presets: 25, 50, 75, 100, 150 — or custom), and optionally assign it to specific athletes (default: the whole roster).
  • Athletes see the target on their Home screen and log reps throughout the week.
  • Progress is live — it recalculates from actual logged reps, never stored separately. A late-syncing rep from a teammate updates the bar instantly.

Receipts

Tap the homework card to see detailed receipts: per-day rep counts, when the last rep was logged, clean-hit rate alongside volume, and whether reps were self-logged or coach-logged.

Advanced

Execution Scoring

For skills assigned a United cheer category (Stunts, Pyramid, Standing Tumbling, Running Tumbling, Tosses, Jumps), HitRate offers an optional execution layer.

How It Works

After logging a rep on an eligible skill, tap the recent-rep chip to open the execution sheet. You'll see the execution drivers for that category (e.g., 4 drivers for stunts/pyramid). All start as HELD — tap any that slipped. Hit Save.

This is strictly binary (held/lost) — no point math. Reps you don't score contribute zero to the execution breakdown (the system never assumes a clean read you didn't give). A green shield chip = all held; amber = something slipped.

The Execution Card on the dashboard shows which drivers slip most often — so you know exactly what to drill.

Tip

Execution scoring is separate from the hit rate, tournament, and cards. It won't inflate your numbers — it's a focused diagnostic tool you opt into per-rep.

Setup & Settings

Managing Your Roster

Add Skills

Tap the editor button (top-right of Home). Add new skills, rename existing ones, reorder by drag, or delete. Assign a United category to enable execution scoring.

Add Athletes

Athletes (subjects) can be added in the editor or on the fly during practice — tap "+" in the counter and name them now or later.

Multiple Folders

Create separate folders for different teams, squads, or seasons from the Folder List (back arrow on Home). Each folder has its own roster and independent stats.

Rename Outcomes

In the editor, customize what each outcome slot is called. "Hit" can become "Clean," "Bobble" can become "Shaky" — whatever language your program uses.

Tip

Tap your team name in the Home header to jump straight to the editor — no need to find the button.

Team Sync

Sharing a Folder

Join Codes

From the Folder List, share a join code with assistant coaches or athletes. When they enter it on their device, they'll join your folder and see the same roster. Reps logged by anyone sync into the shared dashboard — the owner (you) is the source of truth.

Sign in with Apple or Google to back up your data and enable sync. Without an account, everything stays local on your phone.

Get the Most Out of It

Best Practices for Coaches

🎯  Log Consistently

The trend chart and weekly tournament are most useful with regular data. Even 10–20 reps per practice is enough to show meaningful patterns.

📋  Build Practice Pages

If you run the same skills each practice, build a page and use "Run a Page" for faster logging. The 9-pocket grid is purpose-built for routine-based tracking.

📊  Check the Skill Report

The Skill Report card surfaces your best and worst skills automatically. Use it to decide what to drill — the data tells you where the gaps are.

🏆  Use the Tournament

The weekly cup rotation keeps athletes engaged between big competitions. Post the weekly winner on your team's social — the Stunt Cards are designed for exactly this.

🎴  Share Cards After Milestones

When an athlete hits 100 reps, earns a perfect-session milestone, or takes a weekly cup — screenshot and share their card. The card ladder gives athletes something tangible to work toward.

📝  Assign Homework

Set a 50-rep weekly target on a skill that needs work. Athletes can see the progress bar and receipts — it holds everyone accountable without you having to chase.

⌚  Pair Your Watch

If you have an Apple Watch, the companion app lets you log reps from your wrist while you spot or coach on the floor — no phone in hand.
