StuntScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version

StuntScope analyzes your videos on your device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across apps.

Your videos

Videos you record or import, and the pose analysis made from them, stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share analysis with HitRate or turn on "Share Fixes & Stats" in Settings.

Sharing analysis with HitRate (optional)

If you choose to share with HitRate, StuntScope sends the analysis to the HitRate folder you pick: a stability score, per-frame stability data, the skill, and the athletes you select. It does not create a hit or miss rep or change your hit rate. The video itself is never sent. It is stored in HitRate's cloud service (Google Firebase) and is visible to the members of that HitRate team. Use of HitRate is covered by HitRate's own privacy policy.

Improve the model (optional, off by default)

In Settings you can turn on "Share Fixes & Stats". When it is on, StuntScope sends the corrections you make (a skill you change or dismiss, phase timing marks, and the body angles measured at those moments) so we can teach it to read passes more accurately. Each item is tagged with your anonymous user ID (the same random ID described below, not your name). Corrections made while sharing was off stay on your device, but are sent if you turn sharing on later; turning it off deletes any that have not been sent. Video and audio are never sent. You can turn it off at any time and no further corrections are sent after that.

Account and identifiers

StuntScope signs you in anonymously, with no name, email or password. This creates a random user ID, shared with HitRate on your device so your shared analyses appear in your HitRate account. It is used to make sharing work and, if you turn on "Share Fixes & Stats", to tag the corrections you share.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: to record stunts and tumbling.
  • Motion sensors: to steady the pose estimate when the phone moves while recording.
  • Photos (add only): to save analyzed or exported videos to your library.
  • Local network: to connect your own cameras and Mac over Wi-Fi.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, no sale or sharing of data with third parties for their own purposes.

Children

StuntScope is a tool for coaches and athletes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the optional HitRate sharing described above, which is controlled by the coach's team.

Deleting your data

Delete a clip in the app to remove it from your device. To remove a shared analysis from HitRate, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
