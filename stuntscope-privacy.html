StuntScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

StuntScope analyzes your videos on your device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across apps.

Your videos

Videos you record or import, and the pose analysis made from them, stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to log a rep to HitRate.

Logging a rep to HitRate (optional)

If you tap "Log to HitRate", StuntScope sends that one rep to the HitRate team you pick: the clip, a stability score, per-frame stability data, the skill, the outcome you chose, and the athletes you select. It is stored in HitRate's cloud service (Google Firebase) and is visible to the members of that HitRate team. Use of HitRate is covered by HitRate's own privacy policy.

Account and identifiers

StuntScope signs you in anonymously, with no name, email or password. This creates a random user ID, shared with HitRate on your device so your reps appear in your HitRate account. It is used only to make logging work.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: to record stunts and tumbling.
  • Motion sensors: to steady the pose estimate when the phone moves while recording.
  • Photos (add only): to save analyzed or exported videos to your library.
  • Local network: to connect your own cameras and Mac over Wi-Fi.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, no sale or sharing of data with third parties for their own purposes.

Children

StuntScope is a tool for coaches and athletes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the optional HitRate log described above, which is controlled by the coach's team.

Deleting your data

Delete a clip in the app to remove it from your device. To remove a rep logged to HitRate, delete it in HitRate or contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
