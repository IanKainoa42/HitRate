A big 1.1 redesign, built from real practice-floor feedback:

• New folder home — open the app to your folders, tap one to dive into its dashboard.
• Flexible outcomes — define 2–6 custom outcomes per skill with your own labels, colors, and credit weights. Hit rate is now a weighted average, so partial reps count for what they're worth.
• Skills button — jump between skills right from the dashboard.
• Redesigned onboarding — pick your focus areas and get suggested skills to track from the first launch.
• Trash with restore — deleted folders and skills go to Trash instead of vanishing, and your rep history stays safe.
